Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what data Lucide Visuals ("we", "us") handles when you use our website and content service, and exactly what we access through TikTok's official API.

1. Data we access through TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account and authorise our app, we request only two permissions:

  • user.info.basic — your TikTok display name, avatar and open ID (a pseudonymous identifier). We use this only to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload — to deliver finished clips to your TikTok inbox as drafts. We cannot publish on your behalf: every post is published manually by you inside the TikTok app.

We do not read your private messages, your feed, your followers or any analytics from your account.

2. Content material

Footage and branding you supply are processed solely to produce your clips and are stored on our own infrastructure.

3. How we use data

To produce and deliver your clips, to operate and secure the Service, and to communicate with you about your orders. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your content for advertising.

4. Storage and retention

Account link data and clip files are kept on our own servers while your project is active and are removed when the engagement ends or when you ask us to delete them.

5. Your rights

You can revoke our TikTok access at any time from your TikTok account settings. You may also request access, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Third parties

Data is shared only with TikTok (through their official API, to deliver drafts) and with our hosting providers. Nothing else leaves our infrastructure.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository.
